OneFocus Privacy Policy

Operator: OneFocus · Contact: heesankang0830@gmail.com · Last updated: 2026-10-26

What OneFocus collects

  • Account: your Google account email and ID, used only to sign you in (via Supabase Auth).
  • Device usage intervals: for each of your devices, which app was in the foreground, start and end time, and state such as locked, asleep, idle or unknown. On Mac, for Google Chrome, only the hostname of the active tab (for example example.com). Incognito tabs are not recorded.
  • Google Calendar: with the read-only scope calendar.events.readonly, OneFocus reads only the one calendar you designate. From each event it keeps only the event ID, start and end time, last-updated time and whether it was cancelled.

What OneFocus never collects

Keystrokes, screenshots or screen contents, window titles, full URLs (path or query), and calendar event titles, descriptions, attendees or locations.

How it is stored and shared

  • Data is stored on your devices and in a Supabase database (US West, Oregon), sent over TLS. It is not end-to-end encrypted; the operator can technically read stored data.
  • Your Google Calendar access token stays on each device and is never sent to the server.
  • Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than the service providers above (Supabase for storage, Google for sign-in and Calendar).
  • OneFocus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Usage intervals and calendar events are kept for 30 days, then deleted from your devices and the server. You can pause recording on a device at any time. To delete all of your data or your account, email the contact above.
